Should Be Apps

Apps for Mac from Should Be Media. Every download is a zipped app with its SHA-256 checksum. Some apps also need a server that isn't online yet; each card says so.

Opening an app the first time

These apps aren't notarised by Apple yet, so macOS blocks them on first open:
  1. Unzip the download and move the app to your Applications folder.
  2. Open it. When macOS says it can't be opened, click Done.
  3. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll to Security and click Open Anyway, then enter your Mac password.
